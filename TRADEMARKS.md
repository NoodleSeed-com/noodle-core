# Trademark Policy

**Owns:** Permitted community use of Noodle Seed and Noodle Borg names, logos, and product identity.
**Read when:** Naming a fork, integration, event, domain, package, or service related to this project.
**Update when:** Brand ownership or permission rules change.

The Apache-2.0 license grants copyright and patent permissions, not trademark rights. Noodle Seed, Noodle Borg, their logos, and related product identity may be used only as described here or with prior written permission from Noodle Seed.

You may:

- make truthful, non-confusing references such as “compatible with Noodle Seed” or “based on the Noodle Seed open-source project”;
- use the project name to identify the unmodified upstream software; and
- describe contributions, tutorials, reviews, or community events without implying sponsorship.

You may not:

- use a Noodle Seed name or logo as the primary name or branding of a fork, company, hosted service, package, or domain;
- imply endorsement, partnership, certification, or official status without permission;
- use confusingly similar branding or modify a logo for a derivative product; or
- use the marks in a misleading, unlawful, or disparaging way.

Forks should choose a distinct name and state clearly that they are independent. Compatibility wording should be less prominent than the fork's own brand. Requests for exceptions should be sent privately to the repository owner with the proposed use, context, duration, and geography.

This is a practical community policy, not a statement that every listed term is registered in every jurisdiction.
